Languages

From Carepedia, the free encyclopedia

Reminders are available in English, Tamil and Hindi. Medicines never pass through an AI translator: a fixed template keeps the drug name and dose exactly as the doctor wrote them, and only the surrounding words change language. Pick a language to see the same reminder.

Clopidogrel 75 mg · after breakfast · 8:00 AM

The highlighted drug name and dose never change.

All three[edit]

LanguageReminder
EnglishClopidogrel 75 mg · after breakfast · 8:00 AM
TamilClopidogrel 75 mg · காலை உணவுக்குப் பின் · 8:00 AM
HindiClopidogrel 75 mg · नाश्ते के बाद · 8:00 AM

Why drug names stay exact[edit]

A translated drug name can become a different drug, and a reworded dose can become a different dose. Keeping them byte-for-byte as the doctor approved them is the heart of the project.
